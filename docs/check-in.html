Before you arrive

Plan Your Check-In

A little preparation helps us welcome you and your cat smoothly. Complete these steps before your arrival to make check-in quicker and easier.

0 of 5 ready

  1. Sign the online waiver

    Required

    You must sign the boarding waiver and power of attorney for every new booking. Reviewing these documents at home before arrival helps make check-in quicker and smoother for you and your cat(s).

    Sign Waiver

  2. Submit vaccination records

    Required

    Make sure your cat's current vaccination records have been uploaded, emailed or added to their online profile before arrival. We require vaccinations against FVRCP, FeLV, and Rabies for outdoor cats.

    To learn more about our vaccination requirements, please visit our Guidelines section.

  3. Pack food & belongings

    Required

    Bring enough food for the entire stay, plus a little extra, along with treats or familiar items your cat enjoys. Add feeding and medication instructions to your online profile or bring them clearly written on paper so our staff can add them to your cat's file.

    A familiar blanket or scented comfort item is welcome. Corded feeders, fountains and cameras cannot be accepted.

  4. Install the camera app

    We recommend downloading the app before arrival. If the app was previously configured on this device, delete the old configuration or reinstall the app.

    You will receive setup instructions during your arrival by our staff.

    Download App

  5. Complete your final arrival check

    • Every cat must arrive in a secure carrier, with their claws trimmed.
    • Arrive at your confirmed appointment time. Need to change your appointment? Please contact us.
    • New to Alley Cats Hotel? Learn about the normal behaviours cats may show while adjusting to a new environment.
    • Review all guidelines and policies.

You're almost ready.
