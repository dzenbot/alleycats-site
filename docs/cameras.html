Camera Setup Instructions

Watch your cat(s) from your iOS or Android devices.
To get started, follow the simple steps.

  1. Camera credentials

  2. Install the Hik-Connect app

    If the app was previously configured on this device, delete the old configuration or update it with the credentials shown in Step 1.

    Download App

    Camera access is complimentary and intended for monitoring only. The software is provided by a third party and may not be compatible with every device.

  3. Prepare the app

    Open the Hik-Connect app, swipe to Start Now, accept the terms, and select Canada as your region. Location, notification and alert permissions are optional.

  4. Add your room camera

    Tap +, then choose Manual Adding / IP Domain.
    Enter the credentials shown in Step 1 and tap Save.

    Passwords are case-sensitive. We recommend copying and pasting the credentials. Please note that too many attempts will block the device for one hour.

  5. Start watching

    Tap “Start Live View”, then select your room's thumbnail. You're all set!

Enjoy watching your cat(s)!
