Camera Setup Instructions

Watch your cat(s) from your iOS or Android devices.
To get started, please follow the simple steps.

  1. Select your room

    The camera credentials are unique to your room.

  2. Install Hik-Connect

    Tap here to install the free app Hik-Connect for iOS and Android
    If the app was previously configured on this device, delete the old configuration or reinstall the app before continuing.

  3. Prepare the app

    Open the Hik-Connect app, swipe to Start Now, accept the terms, and select Canada as your region. Location, notification and alert permissions are optional.

  4. Add your room camera

    Tap +, then choose Manual Adding → IP/Domain. Enter the credentials shown in Step 1 and tap Save.

    Passwords are case-sensitive. We recommend copying and pasting the credentials. Please note that too many attempts will block the device for one hour.

  5. Start watching

    Tap “Start Live View”, then select your room's thumbnail. You're all set!

Enjoy watching your cat(s)!
