Camera Setup Instructions

To set up Hik-Connect, a third-party camera app, on your phone/tablet (iOS or Android), please follow these simple steps:

  1. Before proceeding, if you already have the Hik-Connect app installed and configured, please delete the previous configuration or uninstall the app.
  2. Download and install the free app by visiting app.alleycatshotel.com or appstore.hikvision.com/0yGe. If your cell network is slow, try our open Wi-Fi AlleyCat-Guest (no password needed).
  3. Open the app. You don’t need to allow permission for location, notifications, or alerts.
  4. Scroll horizontally and press Start Now.
  5. Press Agree for the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
  6. Under Region, select Canada and press OK, or skip entirely.
  7. Press the “+” icon in the top-right corner and choose Manual Adding → IP/Domain.

Select your room to display its camera details.

  1. Press the Save icon in the top-right of the screen.
  2. Finally, press Start Live View.
  3. From the list of thumbnails, find and press the thumbnail for your room.

Enjoy watching your cat(s)!
