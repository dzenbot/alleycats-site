Get Started

Looking to book a stay at the Hotel? Get started in few simple steps:

  1. Create a profile on our website using the Guest Portal
  2. Complete you personal and cat’s information, upload vaccination records, etc.
  3. Create a new reservation with your exact date and time of arrival and departure. Please include the desired room types in the notes area.
  4. Once reviewed by our staff members, you will receive an email confirmation about your booking itinerary, instructions to pay a 50% deposit and a snapshot of our policies and procedures.

The reservation will only be confirmed once the deposit has been paid. It is highly recommended to book in advance for longer stays or during peak holiday seasons

Please visit our FAQ section for some quick answers. If you still have questions or need further assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us.
