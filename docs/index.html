Get Started

Are you new at Alley Cats Hotel? Get started in few simple steps:

  1. Create a user profile on our Guest Portal
  2. Complete you personal and cat’s information, upload vaccination records, etc.
  3. Create a new booking with your exact dates and times of arrival and departure.
    Please include the desired room types in the notes area.
  4. Once reviewed by our staff, you will receive an email confirmation about your booking itinerary and instructions to pay a 50% deposit.

The reservation will only be confirmed once the deposit has been paid. It is highly recommended to book in advance for longer stays or during peak holiday seasons
