Get Started

Are you new at Alley Cats Hotel? Get started in few simple steps:

  1. Create a user profile on our Guest Portal
  2. Complete you personal and cat’s information, upload vaccination records, etc.
  3. Submit your booking with exact arrival and departure dates and times. Add your room preference in the notes (subject to availability).
  4. After we review it, you will receive an email from us about your booking itinerary and instructions to pay a 50% deposit.

The reservation will only be confirmed once the deposit has been paid.
It is highly recommended to book in advance for longer stays or during peak holiday seasons.
