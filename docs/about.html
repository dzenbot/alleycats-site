Our Approach

Our mission is to provide exceptional care for your feline family members in a place that feels like home.

Our cage-free suites are spacious, bright and climate controlled. Private windows, natural light, scratching posts and toys give every guest the freedom to relax in their own way, with views toward the Golden Ears Mountains and Alouette River.

HD cameras and secure door locks help us keep every cat safe. With private access to your cat’s room camera, you can check in anytime from your iOS or Android device whenever you miss them.

Whether you are travelling, renovating, navigating a move or supporting a cat through recovery, our attentive team follows their feeding and medication routines. A veterinarian is on call should an emergency arise.

The result is a calm, attentive stay for your cat, and the peace of mind for you to enjoy your time away.
