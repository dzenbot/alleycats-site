We are situated on 5.5 acres of stunning land in Pitt Meadows, offering a thoughtfully designed and luxurious experience for our fur-guests, as well as peace of mind for their loving parents.

Our mission is to provide exceptional services to your feline family members. Our experienced and attentive staff combined with our unique accommodation options make for an unforgettable experience for both you and your cat(s).

Unlike traditional cat kennels, we offer a cage-free environment where our guests can relax and feel right at home. Our luxury suites are spacious, bright and equipped with individual climate control, private windows, and plenty of natural light. Each suite also includes a scratching post, toys and offers stunning views of the Golden Ears Mountains and the Alouette river.

Our state-of-the-art security system, complete with HD cameras, ensures your cat's safety at all times. You will receive a private access code to monitor your cat's activities from anywhere using either an iOS or Android device.

Many families choose to board their cats with us for various reasons, including vacations, home renovations, recovery from surgery, insurance claims and other major life events. Our staff will adhere to your cat's feeding and medication schedule, and we have a veterinarian on call in case of an emergency.

At Alley Cats Hotel, we aim to provide the best possible experience for your cat, so you can enjoy your time away with peace of mind.
